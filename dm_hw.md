## Overview

Journal article is the one of the most respected outcome of a research. Often for the highest
impact, the challenge for the researchers is to find the best fit journal for the article. With
this project,

1. You will implement a journal finder software tailored to computer science subject
areas. 2. Generate cluster of topics for subject areas
